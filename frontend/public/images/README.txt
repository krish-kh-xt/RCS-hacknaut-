Put the Home screen background photo here and name it:  home-hills.jpg

Use a portrait or wide photo of a hilly / mountain area (misty hills, a road through mountains, dusk or night works best).
Size: about 1080 x 1400 px or larger, under 500 KB so it loads fast. Free photos: unsplash.com or pexels.com.
If this file is missing, the app shows the drawn mountain-and-road scene instead.
